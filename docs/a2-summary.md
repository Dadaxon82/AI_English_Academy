## A2 Day-by-Day Overview

### Day 1: Work and Jobs
- **Grammar**: Third person -s, "would like to"
- **Vocab**: 15 job-related words (job, worker, manager, salary, career)
- **Reading**: Ahmad's job transition story
- **Speaking**: Job description tasks

### Day 2: Daily Routine
- **Grammar**: Past Simple (regular/irregular verbs), adverbs of frequency
- **Vocab**: 15 daily routine words (wake up, brush teeth, go to work)
- **Reading**: Ahmad's daily schedule
- **Speaking**: Describe your routine

### Day 3: Travel and Past Tenses
- **Grammar**: Past Continuous, Present Perfect
- **Vocab**: 15 travel words (journey, arrive, destination, accommodation)
- **Reading**: Ahmad's trip to Uzbekistan
- **Speaking**: Your travel experiences

### Day 4: Future Forms
- **Grammar**: Will, Going to, Future Continuous
- **Vocab**: 15 hobby words (painting, collecting, swimming, dancing)
- **Reading**: Ahmad and Farida's hobbies
- **Speaking**: Future plans

### Day 5: Descriptions
- **Grammar**: Comparatives, Superlatives, "as...as"
- **Vocab**: 15 adjectives (big, small, beautiful, expensive)
- **Reading**: City vs Nature
- **Speaking**: Compare and describe

### Day 6: Advice and Instructions
- **Grammar**: Modals (can, could, should, must)
- **Vocab**: 15 advice words (advice, important, healthy, dangerous)
- **Reading**: Health and safety
- **Speaking**: Give advice

### Day 7: Communication
- **Grammar**: Phrasal verbs, prepositions (at/in/on/for/with)
- **Vocab**: 15 phrasal verbs + 5 prepositions
- **Reading**: Daily conversations
- **Speaking**: Everyday communication
